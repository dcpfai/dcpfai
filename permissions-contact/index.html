Inquiry Categories

Permission Request

Use this category when your proposed use may go beyond the permissions already granted on the Copyright & Important Notice page.

DCPF Implementation Question

Ask about understanding or applying DCPF, its four frameworks, its standard responsibilities, or the public starter workflow.

Research or Academic Inquiry

Use this category for scholarly discussion, research questions, classroom use, citation questions, study proposals, or academic collaboration.

Media Inquiry

Use this category for interviews, articles, podcasts, broadcasts, quotations, background information, or other media requests.

Collaboration

Describe a proposed project, evaluation, domain application, educational effort, or other collaboration involving DCPF.

Other

Use this category when none of the listed options accurately describes your inquiry.

What to Prepare for a Permission Request

Before the form becomes available, gather the details needed to evaluate the proposed use:

  • the specific DCPF material, passage, figure, template, or artifact you want to use;
  • how and where the material would be reproduced, adapted, displayed, taught, or distributed;
  • the purpose, format, intended audience, and expected distribution;
  • whether the proposed use is commercial or noncommercial;
  • whether you plan to modify, translate, excerpt, or combine the material with other content;
  • the name of the person or organization making the request;
  • any relevant publication, event, project, or decision deadline; and
  • your contact information.
Submitting a request will not itself grant permission. Permission is granted only through an explicit written response identifying the approved use and any applicable conditions.

What to Include in Other Inquiries

Provide enough context to understand your question or proposal without including confidential, proprietary, sensitive, or regulated information. Helpful details may include:

  • the inquiry category and a concise subject;
  • your question, proposed activity, or desired outcome;
  • the DCPF page, framework, responsibility, artifact, or publication involved;
  • your domain or intended setting;
  • what you have already tried or reviewed; and
  • any relevant, reasonable response deadline.

Before Requesting Permission

Some uses may already be addressed by the current published terms. Review the Copyright & Important Notice before preparing a request.

dcpfai.org is the authoritative site and definitive reference for current official DCPF materials, permissions information, and updates. Do not rely on an older or third-party copy when identifying material for a request.

Important Boundaries

  • This page does not provide legal, medical, financial, safety, or other professional advice.
  • DCPF is experimental and has not been broadly or independently validated.
  • An implementation question does not transfer responsibility for your design, content, validation, decisions, or outcomes.
  • Do not send passwords, private keys, personal records, unpublished client data, or other sensitive information through the forthcoming general inquiry form.
