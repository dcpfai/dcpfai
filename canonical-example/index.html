Canonical Example Implementation

This example shows a shortened DCPF implementation for a narration about Old Faithful and Yellowstone’s hydrothermal setting.

These website artifacts are intentionally compact. For the complete artifacts, detailed templates, execution examples, and training practicums, refer to the current DCPF Introductory Edition when its reader-ready PDF becomes available.

View Downloads and Official Materials

Project Goal: Create a short, accurate, listener-friendly narration explaining Old Faithful and its hydrothermal setting in Yellowstone National Park.

1. Governance

The Governance Framework guides the project. As recurring controls become useful beyond one artifact or execution, they are preserved in the Governance Repository.

Governance Repository Excerpt | NPN-GOV-001 Project: National Park Narration Owner: Project Editor Status: Active Framework Purpose Establish the standards and boundaries for accurate, accessible, and responsible national park narrations. Human authority remains with the Project Editor. Quality Standards • Material factual claims must be supported by approved evidence and must not be stated with greater certainty than the evidence allows. • Public narrations must explain the subject accurately without distorting important scientific meaning for simplification. Approved Information Sources • Research may use official National Park Service, United States Geological Survey, Yellowstone Volcano Observatory, other relevant government, and appropriate authoritative sources. • Directly relevant official or primary evidence should be preferred when reasonably available. Prohibited Content • Do not invent factual claims, conceal material uncertainty, present unsupported inference as established fact, or generate unsupported real-time visitor guidance or eruption predictions. Validation Requirements • Research findings must be checked before acceptance as Knowledge. • Public-facing Project Outputs require human editorial review before release. Output Standards • Use a calm, clear, approachable presentation for a general adult audience. • Explain technical concepts in ordinary language before specialized terminology when needed. Revision History Version 1.0 | Initial active Governance for the National Park Narration project | Approved by the Project Editor

The Research Request applies the relevant Governance controls where they affect the investigation. It does not depend on the Governance Repository being attached or supplied separately.

2. Research

Research Request | NPN-RES-001 Framework Purpose Research Old Faithful and its hydrothermal setting for a future public narration. This is Research, not a request to write the final narration. Research Configuration Variables Yellowstone National Park | Old Faithful | General adult audience | Introductory depth Research Objectives Explain geyser conditions and processes; place Old Faithful in Yellowstone’s hydrothermal system; define essential terms; identify misconceptions and uncertainty; identify stable safety context. Approved Information Sources National Park Service, Yellowstone National Park, United States Geological Survey, Yellowstone Volcano Observatory, other relevant government sources, and appropriate authoritative educational or professional sources. Source Priority Hierarchy Prefer the most direct, current, and relevant official or primary evidence reasonably available for the claim. Preserve material source conflicts. Research Scope Include hydrothermal processes, Old Faithful context, essential terminology, misconceptions, and stable safety information. Exclude real-time eruption predictions, current travel planning, detailed itineraries, unsupported folklore, and restricted or unvalidated material. Output Organization Executive summary; findings by objective; evidence and uncertainty notes; unresolved or excluded material; source register; completion statement. Research Source Documentation Preserve enough source information for later verification. Do not invent missing citation details. Sequential Output Requirements If needed, continue across responses at natural stopping points without compressing or omitting required Research. Research Boundary The Research Output is evidence for human review. It does not become Accepted Knowledge automatically.
Research produces evidence. Human review decides what is accepted for future use.

3. Knowledge

Knowledge Repository | NPN-KNW-001 Status: Approved for Runtime use Framework Purpose Preserve the approved information used to develop a short, listener-friendly narration about Old Faithful and Yellowstone’s hydrothermal setting. Knowledge Configuration Variables Subject: Old Faithful | Audience: general adult listener | Use: approved findings only Accepted Knowledge Hydrothermal System: Rain and snow supply groundwater that moves through faults and fractures. At depth, magma and hot rock heat the water. The heated water rises through a hidden plumbing system. For a general audience, the essentials are water, heat, and a pathway through rock. How a Geyser Erupts: A geyser is a hot spring with constrictions in its plumbing. Deep water becomes very hot under pressure. As heated water rises, steam forms and expands until water and steam are forced from the vent. Old Faithful in Context: Old Faithful is part of Yellowstone’s larger hydrothermal system and is regular enough to support predictions, but it does not erupt on an exact hourly timetable. Stable Safety Context: Visitors in thermal areas should remain on boardwalks and designated trails and should not touch thermal features or runoff. Current conditions and regulations should be checked with the National Park Service. Excluded Material: Current eruption predictions, live park conditions, travel planning, detailed visitor itineraries, and unvalidated claims are not part of this Accepted Knowledge. Information Categories Hydrothermal System; How a Geyser Erupts; Old Faithful in Context; Stable Safety Context; Excluded Material; Source Register Acceptance Note The Project Editor reviewed and approved this Knowledge for the stated Runtime use. Material changes require human review.
Runtime receives the complete approved Knowledge and the current execution instructions.

4. Runtime

Runtime Request | NPN-RUN-001 Framework Purpose Use the approved Knowledge to create a short, accurate, listener-friendly narration. Do not conduct new Research or add factual information from general model knowledge. Runtime Configuration Variables Audience: general adult listener | Length: approximately two minutes | Tone: calm, clear, approachable, curious | Format: continuous spoken narration Input Knowledge Supply the complete approved NPN-KNW-001 content. If the Knowledge is missing, incomplete, or unreadable, stop and request it. Task Definition Introduce Old Faithful within Yellowstone’s hydrothermal setting; explain water, underground heat, rock pathways, constricted plumbing, pressure, and steam; explain that Old Faithful is predictable but not on an exact hourly timetable; include a brief stable safety statement. Output Requirements Return a narration draft and a short internal validation note. Preserve the approved facts and qualifications. Do not add unsupported current guidance or eruption predictions. Sequential Output Requirements If the complete Project Output cannot fit in one response, continue at natural stopping points without compression or omission. Validation Check the output against the Task Definition, complete approved Knowledge, required components, terminology, and output conditions. Human editorial review is required before public use.

Runtime produces a Project Output. Human review decides whether it is accepted, revised, rejected, or routed back to the responsibility that owns a problem.

5. Project Output and Human Review

Project Output Excerpt Old Faithful is one visible expression of Yellowstone’s much larger hydrothermal system. Water from rain and snow moves underground through cracks and fractures, where heat from deep below warms it. In a geyser, constricted underground plumbing helps trap that hot water under pressure. As steam forms and expands, water and steam are eventually forced upward in an eruption. Old Faithful is predictable, but it does not erupt on an exact hourly schedule.

Before public use, the narration is reviewed against the approved Knowledge, Runtime requirements, and applicable project standards.

Governance guides. Research discovers. Human review accepts. Knowledge preserves. Runtime executes. Human review closes the cycle.

Experimental Use Boundary

DCPF is experimental. Test and validate your own implementation in a controlled, low-risk environment before considering real-world or production use.
