Copyright & Important Notice

What the Introductory Edition Will Add

The website provides the practical starting path. The complete Introductory Edition adds deeper explanation and extended learning resources, including:

  • the full conceptual and architectural development of DCPF;
  • detailed Governance, Research, Knowledge, and Runtime chapters;
  • guided practicums and worked examples;
  • full template starts and implementation guidance;
  • quick-reference, checklist, glossary, and responsibility-consequence resources; and
  • additional discussion of validation, maintenance, scaling, and future development.

Before You Download or Use DCPF Materials

  • Read the Copyright & Important Notice.
  • Remember that DCPF is experimental and has not been broadly or independently validated.
  • Begin in a controlled, low-risk testing environment.
  • Validate the implementation for your domain, AI system, tools, and operating environment.
  • Retain human judgment, verification, acceptance, and accountability.
  • Confirm that the file comes from dcpfai.org and is identified as the current official edition.

Need a Permission Beyond the Published Terms?

Requests involving reproduction, publication, training materials, commercial reuse, media, research, collaboration, or another use beyond the stated permissions can be submitted through the forthcoming Permissions & Contact page.
