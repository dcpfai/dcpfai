Why Support DCPF?

DCPF and the Introductory Edition are being made freely available so that individuals, educators, researchers, professionals, and organizations can examine and test the approach without a purchase requirement.

Voluntary support, including the simple idea of buying us a coffee, can help sustain the practical work required to keep an independent public project available and useful.

Website and Distribution

Domain registration, website hosting, secure delivery, and the continued availability of current official materials.

Accessible Learning Materials

Readable website content, accessible document preparation, clear diagrams, mobile presentation, and materials designed for both human and AI-assisted learning.

Testing and Refinement

Controlled experiments, cross-domain applications, documented examples, corrections, and responsible refinement of the framework.

Future Editions

Maintenance of the Introductory Edition, starter resources, templates, translations, and future educational materials.

Support Is Voluntary

  • Official DCPF materials identified as free will remain available without requiring a contribution.
  • Contributing will not provide certification, endorsement, special permission, implementation approval, or influence over DCPF conclusions.
  • A contribution will not transfer responsibility for how DCPF is adapted, validated, or used.
  • Unless explicitly stated by the future payment provider and applicable law, do not assume that a contribution is tax deductible.
Access to the framework and permission to use DCPF materials are governed by the published terms, not by whether someone provides financial support.

Ways to Support DCPF Now

You can support the project before a financial option is available:

  • share dcpfai.org as the authoritative source rather than redistributing incomplete or outdated material;
  • use the framework first in controlled, low-risk testing environments;
  • retain human review and report clearly when an application is experimental;
  • cite DCPF accurately and preserve the framework’s stated limits;
  • review the Copyright & Important Notice before reproducing or adapting materials; and
  • prepare constructive implementation or research feedback for the forthcoming Contact & Permissions form.

Transparency and Authoritative Information

dcpfai.org is the authoritative site and definitive reference for official DCPF materials, project updates, permissions information, and any future support method.

Do not send money through an account, message, or third-party page that is not linked from this official Support DCPF page. When a support option is activated, this notice will be replaced with clear instructions.
